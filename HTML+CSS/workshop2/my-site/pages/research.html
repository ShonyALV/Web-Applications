Current Research Work

I am currently working as a research assistant on three different projects:

Guided by PhD Rigoberto Fonseca

EEG Pattern Detection with Coupled Functions

This project focuses on detecting patterns in EEG signals using coupled functions. I am conducting this research under the guidance of PhD Rigoberto Fonseca.

Guided by PhD Juan Pablo Astudillo

Kichwa to Spanish Translator

This project involves Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques to build a translator from Kichwa to Spanish. I am working on this research with PhD Juan Pablo Astudillo.

Guided by PhD Israel Pineda & PhD Scott Williams

Climate Prediction using Evapotranspiration

This project explores climate prediction through evapotranspiration models, applying Self-Organizing Maps (SOM) and High-Performance Computing (HPC). I am collaborating with PhD Israel Pineda and PhD Scott Williams.
